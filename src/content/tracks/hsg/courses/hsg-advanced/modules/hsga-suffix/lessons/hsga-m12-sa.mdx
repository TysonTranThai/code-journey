# Order, then count

Suffix-array construction by rank doubling is O(n log^2 n) with a plain
sort — fast enough for n = 2000 course loads. Kasai's algorithm walks
the text left-to-right, each step's LCP dropping at most one: O(n)
total. The counting identity n(n+1)/2 - Σ LCP falls out of how sorted
suffixes share prefixes.
